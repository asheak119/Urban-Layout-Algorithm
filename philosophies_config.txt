[PHILOSOPHY_01]
id = hippodamian_grid
name = Classical Hippodamian Grid
topology = Orthogonal rectilinear grid aligned to fixed cardinal or diagonal axes.
block_pattern = Elongated rectangles (approx. 2:1 to 3:1 ratio).
anchors = Central Agora (occupying 1-2 full blocks); Acropolis placed on adjacent high ground if present.
density = Medium density; uniform street-wall setbacks.
slope_tolerance = Moderate
terrain_behavior = Flat/gentle terrain runs unhindered. Moderate cross-slopes convert transverse streets to stepped pedestrian paths. Fails if unbuildable block ratio exceeds slope slider limit. Cliffs truncate grid abruptly without curving. Water terminates in right-angle quays.

[PHILOSOPHY_02]
id = roman_castrum
name = Ancient Roman Castrum
topology = Rectangular walled perimeter centered on two primary cross-axes (Cardo Maximus N-S, Decumanus Maximus E-W). Secondary orthogonal infill per quadrant.
block_pattern = Small to moderate square insulae.
anchors = Forum at central intersection of Cardo and Decumanus; fortified gates at the 4 axis terminuses.
density = High density; continuous perimeter walls and row structures.
slope_tolerance = Gentle to Moderate
terrain_behavior = Requires heavy cut-and-fill to maintain rigid axes. Invalidates if primary cross-axes exceed slope tolerance. Natural cliffs replace defensive perimeter walls; interior cliffs cause generation failure.

[PHILOSOPHY_03]
id = laws_of_the_indies
name = Laws of the Indies (Spanish Colonial)
topology = Orthogonal grid radiating outward from an expansive central plaza. 4 midpoint streets and 8 corner streets extend outward to disperse prevailing winds.
block_pattern = Uniform square or slightly rectangular blocks.
anchors = Plaza Mayor at center; main Cathedral on east edge; Cabildo/administrative palace on opposing edges.
density = Medium-high density; courtyard buildings sharing party walls with zero front setback.
slope_tolerance = Moderate
terrain_behavior = Plaza Mayor requires level ground (invalidates if plaza elevation variance exceeds 15% of slope threshold). Outer blocks absorb slope via stepped foundations. Streets truncate cleanly at cliff edges without curving. Port layouts anchor the plaza 1 block inland from the shoreline.

[PHILOSOPHY_04]
id = star_fort

topology = Concentric polygonal rings intersecting radial avenues projecting from the center to bastion points.
block_pattern = Trapezoidal blocks narrowing toward the center.
anchors = Central polygonal Piazza d'Armi; military barracks/arsenals distributed along defensive perimeter bastions.
density = Uniform height and continuous facades; surrounded by a wide, unbuilt exterior glacis.

[PHILOSOPHY_05]
id = baroque_axial
name = Baroque Axial / Grand Vista
topology = Diagonal tri-radial avenues (trivium) and star-burst axes slicing through the terrain toward monumental focal points; secondary infill fills triangular wedges.
block_pattern = Large, irregular triangular and trapezoidal wedge blocks.

[PHILOSOPHY_06]
id = penn_grid
name = Quaker Enlightenment Grid (Penn Plan)
topology = Uniform rectangular grid bounded between two natural geographic boundaries (e.g., rivers); bisected by two wide primary axes.
block_pattern = Large square or rectangular blocks subdivided by narrow interior alleys.
anchors = Center Square at the primary intersection (City Hall); 4 symmetrically placed public garden squares in quadrants.
density = Detached and semi-detached residential/commercial parcels with generous yard allocations.
slope_tolerance = Moderate
terrain_behavior = Grid continues across rolling terrain; slopes absorbed via stepped basement cuts. Generation fails if the two main bisecting axes cannot cross the terrain within the slope threshold. Bridges cross bounding water bodies perpendicularly.

[PHILOSOPHY_07]
id = savannah_ward
name = Cellular Ward System (Savannah Plan)
topology = Modular, repeating civic cells (wards). Each ward consists of a central square flanked by 4 civic trust lots (E/W) and 40 residential tything lots (N/S).
block_pattern = Repeating cellular units bounded by peripheral avenues.
anchors = Central green square in every ward; schools, churches, and public halls on flanking trust lots.
density = Balanced medium density; residential rowhouses surrounding communal green space.
slope_tolerance = Gentle
terrain_behavior = Highly sensitive to elevation changes. Individual wards fail to spawn if internal elevation exceeds gentle thresholds. Fails entirely if fewer than 4 contiguous wards can be placed. Designed to anchor along elevated river bluffs or coastlines.

[PHILOSOPHY_08]
id = commissioners_grid
name = Speculative Utilitarian Grid (1811 Plan)
topology = High-density orthogonal grid: wide, broadly spaced longitudinal avenues intersected by closely spaced transverse cross-streets.
block_pattern = Long, narrow rectangular blocks (approx. 4:1 to 5:1 ratio).
anchors = Commercial activity distributed continuously along avenues; cross-streets reserved for residential infill. Minimal civic plazas.
density = Maximum floor area ratio; zero front/side setbacks.
slope_tolerance = Steep
terrain_behavior = Imposes grid through heavy civil grading. Depressions are filled and hills leveled. Steep slopes between avenues are resolved via retaining walls or extreme street grades up to the slider limit. Cliffs cause abrupt dead ends or elevation severing. Terminus at water features finger piers and docks.

[PHILOSOPHY_09]
id = jeffersonian_macro
name = Section-Line Macro Grid (PLSS)
topology = Macro-arterial grid spaced on uniform, large intervals (mile/half-mile scale). Interiors are populated independently with secondary local grids, strips, or clusters.
block_pattern = Massive macro-blocks containing inward-facing sub-developments.
anchors = Commercial nodes, high schools, and retail centers placed at four-way macro intersections.
density = Low-to-moderate; low-density residential interior, auto-oriented commercial along perimeter.
slope_tolerance = Steep
terrain_behavior = Macro arterials run continuously; if a slope threshold is exceeded, the arterial deviates locally and snaps back to the section line once clear. Cliffs trigger switchbacks or terminus. Long-span bridges cross water at section lines.

[PHILOSOPHY_13]
id = terraced_contour
name = Terraced Contour / Hillside Urbanism
topology = Horizontal parallel roads tracing contour lines (iso-elevation paths), linked vertically by switchback ramps, stairs, funiculars, or elevators.
block_pattern = Long, thin horizontal ribbons terraced into the slope face.
anchors = Artificial platforms carved from hillsides, or transit stations at upper/lower terminus points.
density = Staggered building volumes where rooftops double as terraces for upper levels.
slope_tolerance = Extreme
terrain_behavior = Requires uneven/mountainous terrain (ineffective on flat ground). Roads maintain near-zero grades by hugging contours. When cliff thresholds are hit, roads turn into half-tunnels or anchor via pilings/retaining walls. Coastal variants terminate at sea-level harbors.

[PHILOSOPHY_18]
id = tod_radial
name = Transit-Oriented Radial Development (TOD)
topology = Concentric, fine-grained walkable street grid centered within a 400m to 800m radius of a central rail transit hub. High intersection density and dedicated pedestrian paths.
block_pattern = Small, walkable blocks (60-100m).
anchors = Mass transit station (light rail or heavy rail) in the central plaza.
density = Highest density (mixed-use commercial/residential) within 400m of station; tapers to townhouses and detached residential at outer edge.
slope_tolerance = Moderate
terrain_behavior = Optimal on gentle slopes to maintain walkability. Moderate slopes use switchback pedestrian ramps or public stairs/elevators. Invalidation occurs if a cliff or barrier severs more than 40% of the 800m walking radius. Waterfronts incorporate ferry terminals.

block_pattern = Large, non-geometric parcels following natural terrain boundaries.
anchors = Geographic landmarks: central lakes, communal greens, or preserved woodland groves.
